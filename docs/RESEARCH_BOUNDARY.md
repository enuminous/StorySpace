# StorySpace: research boundary

**Status (2026-11-09): literature-grounded conceptual research scaffolding, NOT measured retrocausality.**

## The distinction

The **experience** is an introspective event: "this felt familiar", "I thought it was about to happen", or "I dreamed something like this". A person's report deserves to be recorded as such.

The **interpretation** is a proposed mechanism: recognition without recollection, familiarity/fluency attribution, predictive processing, selective recall, post-selection, coincidence, or (more speculatively) reverse-time causal influence.

The **evidence** would be a separately defined observation under a frozen, independently verifiable rule that could discriminate among alternatives. A subjective sensation does not carry its preferred explanation as a physical measurement.

### Working vocabulary (not ontology)

- **Signalspace**: self-reported sensations, intensity, timing and context.
- **Storyspace**: hypotheses and post-hoc narratives built to explain those experiences.
- **Evidencespace**: independently verifiable predictions, counterfactual controls, decisions and provenance.
- **Retrospace**: our name for exploration of time-symmetric mathematical formalisms; **not** an established physical region or a proven signal from the future.

## What's actually known

Cognitive research supports familiarity-without-recollection and recognition-memory conflict as plausible explanations for at least some déjà vu experiences. A 2018 study by Cleary and Claxton reported an *illusion of prediction*: people in induced déjà vu states felt able to predict a next turn but did not predict better than chance on the tested navigation task. These specific results do not establish that every form of apparent premonition has the same cause.

Quantum delayed-choice experiments are real quantum-optics observations. In standard quantum-mechanical operational analyses, coincident/conditionally selected subsets can display patterns not visible in the earlier unconditioned marginal. This does not create a channel for controllable information to be sent into the past. Time-symmetric and retrocausal *interpretations* remain subjects of foundational debate; this repository neither rules them all out nor presents experimental proof for them.

### Hard exclusions

A forecast written down after the outcome is **retrospective narrative**, never prospective prediction evidence.

A Git/browser timestamp generated on a machine controlled by the experimenter is **not** independent time custody. A hash proves content identity under stated assumptions, not necessarily when it existed.

A single impressive match, an unusual dream, déjà vu intensity, or an artificial quantum-filter graph **cannot** by itself substantiate a physical retrocausal mechanism.

### Appropriate program language

**PASS:** a specifically defined gate is met within its scope.  
**FAIL:** a specifically defined claim is contradicted / criterion unmet.  
**UNRESOLVED:** the necessary evidence is not available.  
**SIMULATED:** examples in the interface were generated for illustration.  
**OBSERVED:** a report or instrument measurement occurred; distinguish who observed what.

**No live human experiment is claimed.** SS-001 has not yet been independently preregistered or executed.

A note on personal well-being: unusual feelings of familiarity are common; frequent or distressing changes in perception merit discussion with a qualified clinician, not a retrocausality inference.
