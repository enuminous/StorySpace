Why this matters

Imagine two parties later compare matching detection records. They can recover conditional structures invisible in the unsorted counts. That does not change the marginal distribution available to the earlier observer. The experiment must be analyzed with its real instruments, timing, selection rules and no-signaling constraints—not by treating coincidence sorting as a rewrite of history.
